FAQ

Questions before you start.

What does Celestl do?

Celestl is a technical execution partner. We handle the engineering for AI products, agents, automation, SaaS, MVPs and custom software.

What technologies do you use?

OpenAI and other LLMs, Python, FastAPI, React, Next.js, n8n, PostgreSQL, vector databases and cloud infrastructure. The stack is chosen for each project.

Do you work with agencies and consultants?

Yes. We can work as a white-label team behind the scenes, or as a referral or delivery partner.

Is my project kept confidential?

Yes. We can sign an NDA, and confidential client work is never shown publicly without permission.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing engagements, retainers and extended engineering support.
