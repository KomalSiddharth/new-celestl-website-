The challenge: leads handled by hand

Every enquiry, whether it came from the website form, email or a message, went through the same manual routine. Someone read it, decided how serious it was, forwarded it to the right person and then typed the details into the CRM.

It worked while volume was low. As enquiries grew, replies got slower, good leads went cold while they waited, and some were never logged at all. The team was spending its best hours on sorting instead of selling.

Our approach

  1. Map the real process. We sat with the team and wrote down every step a lead goes through, including the judgement calls people made without noticing.
  2. Turn judgement into rules. We defined what makes a lead hot, warm or not a fit, using past enquiries as examples.
  3. Build and test on real leads. The workflow ran alongside the team for a short period so we could compare its decisions with theirs.
  4. Hand over. Once the tags matched, the manual routine was switched off and the team was shown how to adjust the rules themselves.

What we built: an AI lead routing workflow

One automation that takes a lead from first message to CRM record:

  • Reads every incoming lead and pulls out name, need, budget and urgency.
  • Tags it by intent, so hot leads are obvious at a glance.
  • Routes it to the right person and notifies them straight away.
  • Creates or updates the CRM record, with the original message attached.
  • Flags anything unclear for a human instead of guessing.

Is lead automation right for your business?

If your team reads and sorts enquiries by hand, replies depend on who is free, or your CRM is always a little out of date, this kind of workflow usually pays for itself quickly. It works with the tools you already use.

Common questions

Which CRMs can this work with?

Most modern CRMs with an API, including HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive and Salesforce, as well as simpler setups like Airtable or Google Sheets.

Does the AI make the final decision on a lead?

No. It sorts, tags and routes. Your team decides what to do with each lead, and anything unclear is sent to a person.
