The challenge: a store that was slow on mobile

The store looked fine on a laptop, but most customers were shopping on their phones. Pages took too long to load, and the theme carried years of leftover apps, scripts and code that nobody used any more.

The bigger problem was between the cart and payment. Too many steps and too much clutter meant shoppers who wanted to buy were leaving before they finished.

Our approach

  1. Audit before touching anything. We measured page speed and went through every app and script to see what was needed and what was only slowing things down.
  2. Design for the phone first. Product pages, cart and checkout were laid out for a small screen, then adapted for desktop.
  3. Rebuild the theme, clean. Instead of patching the old theme, we built a lighter one with only what the store uses.
  4. Test the buying journey. We went through the full purchase on real devices before launch, then moved the store over with no downtime.

What we built: a faster Shopify theme and checkout

A rebuilt storefront the brand fully owns:

  • A lightweight custom theme with unused apps and scripts removed.
  • Product pages that load quickly and put price, variants and the buy button first.
  • A shorter cart and checkout with fewer steps and fewer distractions.
  • Optimised images that stay sharp without slowing the page.
  • Sections the brand team can edit without a developer.

Is a Shopify rebuild right for your store?

If most of your traffic is mobile but most of your sales are not, if your theme has collected apps over the years, or if shoppers add to cart and then disappear, a rebuild is usually worth more than another round of patches.

Common questions

Will my store go offline during the rebuild?

No. The new theme is built and tested separately, then published in one switch.

Do I lose my products, orders or SEO rankings?

No. Products, orders and customer data stay as they are, and URLs are kept the same so search rankings are protected.
